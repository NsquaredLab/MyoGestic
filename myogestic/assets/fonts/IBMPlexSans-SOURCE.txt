IBM Plex Sans Regular, static TrueType, font version 3.005.
Unmodified complete font from IBM's official @ibm/plex-sans@1.1.0 release:
https://github.com/IBM/plex/releases/tag/%40ibm/plex-sans%401.1.0
https://github.com/IBM/plex/releases/download/%40ibm/plex-sans%401.1.0/ibm-plex-sans.zip

Archive paths:
ibm-plex-sans/fonts/complete/ttf/IBMPlexSans-Regular.ttf
ibm-plex-sans/LICENSE.txt (shipped as IBMPlexSans-OFL.txt)

The complete face includes Latin, Greek, Cyrillic, arrows and scientific symbols.
Only Regular is used by the app; icons are merged from Font Awesome 6.

The accompanying licence text is unchanged; line endings and trailing whitespace
are normalized for this repository.
